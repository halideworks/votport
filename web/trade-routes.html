Connected ports

How addresses, permissions and delivery status work

A port address tells VOTPort where to connect. A fingerprint verifies the port’s identity. An approved invitation grants permission to send files to one receiving endpoint.

“Received and verified” means the files arrived intact. “Released” means the receiving workflow also finished; it may first need processing or approval. Pausing or revoking a route never deletes previously received files.

For a one-off upload, open the receiving team’s ordinary receive link in your browser.
